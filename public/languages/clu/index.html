About CLU

CLU is a programming language. It is a statically typed language. It supports imperative and object-oriented programming.

CLU first appeared in 1975 and was designed by Barbara Liskov. Development is led by Massachusetts Institute of Technology.

How CLU is implemented

In the Language Lineage dataset, its compiler is written in Assembly.

CLU in the language family tree

CLU drew on ideas from ALGOL 60, Lisp, and Simula and went on to influence Rust and Python.
