About Modula-3

Modula-3 is a programming language. It is a statically typed language. It supports imperative, object-oriented, and concurrent programming.

Modula-3 first appeared in 1988. Development is led by Digital Equipment Corporation.

How Modula-3 is implemented

In the Language Lineage dataset, its compiler is written in C.

Modula-3 in the language family tree

Modula-3 drew on ideas from Modula-2 and went on to influence Python.
