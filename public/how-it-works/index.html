How It Works

Language Lineage is built on a rigorous pipeline that combines the scale of AI agents with the absolute accuracy of human experts.

🤖

1. Autonomous AI Agents Scrape & Gather

Specialized AI agents continuously monitor public repositories, Wikidata, Wikipedia, and compiler source trees. They gather raw unstructured data, historical release notes, and complex repository structures at a scale humans couldn't manage alone.

🧠

2. LLM Processing & Structuring

The raw data is passed through advanced Large Language Models. These models parse natural language, resolve conflicting historical dates, and map complex relationships (like compiler bootstrapping chains) into our strict, strongly-typed JSON dataset schema.

👨‍💻

3. Human Verification & Curation

AI isn't perfect, so humans have the final say. Every single implementation edge, release date, and lineage connection is manually verified by human researchers before it goes live. This ensures the atlas remains an authoritative, evidence-backed record of software history.
